## Editorial decisions

International examples on PDF p. 7 are inspiration, not TDL client projects, and are not included in the built portfolio. The photo collection uses client supplied photography for Olkai, MESOB and workshop fabrication. The invalid printed underscore domain is not presented as an active website; no unverified domain or email is invented. Broad claims about client satisfaction, budgets, decades of experience and compliance with unnamed standards are not amplified into guarantees, testimonials, statistics or certifications. Project years use catalogue Gregorian values. Amharic and Afaan Oromoo interface/content updated in parallel; proper project names remain in their published form. Translations require native editorial review for launch.

## Verification

All 3 language dictionaries contain 4 real projects, 6 services and 5 process steps. Original project photography extracted from PDF image objects without promotional text overlays. Catalogue download embedded. Theme, navigation, image viewer, gallery controls and inquiry drafting retained. Contact form remains local drafting and copy only, without sending a message.
